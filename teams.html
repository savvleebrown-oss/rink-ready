Teams & Follows

Your favorite team does not need a complicated origin story. A city, a player, or a jersey you love is enough.

Three ways to choose

Start with a place

Try a team near your home, campus, or a city you love. Local coverage and nearby fans can make following the season easier.

Start with a player

Watch highlights and interviews. A creative passer, fearless goalie, or leader with a story you connect to can become your reason to tune in.

Start with a game

Watch two teams without choosing a side. Notice whose style draws you in. You can follow more than one team and change your mind.

Explore both leagues

Meet NHL teams

The NHL includes teams across the United States and Canada. Browse its official team directory for club websites, rosters, and schedules.

Try comparing one nearby team with one whose players interest you. You do not need to memorize the whole league.

Explore the NHL team directory

Discover the PWHL

The PWHL showcases elite professional women’s hockey. Explore its current teams, player profiles, and highlights through the league’s official website.

Pay attention to the players’ stories as well as the scores. Following women athletes can make the sport feel more connected to your own interests.

Explore the PWHL

Build a hockey feed worth opening

  • One league account: Use social links from the NHL or PWHL official website to find authentic announcements and highlights.
  • One team account: Follow your chosen club for lineups, game updates, and behind-the-scenes moments.
  • One player: Look for a profile linked by the player’s team or league rather than trusting a similar username.
  • One thoughtful explainer: Choose a writer or creator who cites sources, explains mistakes, and welcomes beginner questions.

League and team links above lead to real organizations. Her Rink is a fictional class project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.

A community should make room for you

Look for fan groups with clear conduct rules and active moderation. You should be able to ask what icing means without being mocked. Leave spaces that gatekeep, harass, or pressure you to share personal information.

Interested in playing? Ask a local rink about adult beginner sessions, women’s programs, equipment requirements, and rental options. Availability and costs vary, so check before signing up.

Your next step can be small: pick one game, invite a friend, and see what catches your attention.
