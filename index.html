Her Rink

A welcoming starting point for women learning hockey, one faceoff, favorite player, and game night at a time.

You do not need to have played, own a jersey, or know every rule to enjoy hockey. Start with what interests you: the speed, the teamwork, the rivalries, or the women making their mark on the ice.

Meet our fictional editors

Her Rink and its editors are invented for this class project. The hockey explanations describe the real sport.

Maya Bennett · The rule translator

Maya became a fan after watching a playoff game with her roommate. She turns confusing whistles into everyday explanations.

Elena Brooks · The game-night planner

Elena loves women’s hockey and the atmosphere of a crowded rink. Her favorite advice: bring a layer and ask the question.

One small goal for your first game

Notice when possession changes, watch how players pass into open space, and pick one player to follow during a shift. Understanding everything can wait. Enjoying the game cannot.
